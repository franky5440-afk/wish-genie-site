Version 2.4

November 29, 2026

  • Sources in Ask AI: turn on "Add sources to answers" when you start a conversation. The AI searches a set of medical sites and links to what it used. It's off by default, and each question costs more on your OpenRouter account.
  • Links in AI answers can be tapped only when they came from that search. Other links show as plain text.
  • Sources use the search service Exa, so you'll be asked to agree once more before your next question.

Version 2.3

November 27, 2026

  • Save to a folder you choose: iCloud Drive and Dropbox destinations now have "Save to". Pick "A folder I choose" and any folder you already have, so files land where your own tools already look. Renaming or moving the folder is fine.
  • iCloud Drive: folders in iCloud Drive or On My iPhone. Dropbox: folders in Dropbox, with the Dropbox app installed and signed in on this iPhone.
  • If the folder is deleted, the task tells you, and you can choose it again.

Version 2.2

November 25, 2026

  • ECG: a new group in "Pick my own" exports your Apple Watch ECG recordings as they appear in Health, one record each (result, average heart rate, symptoms and sampling rate). Turn on "Include voltage waveform" to add the waveform to JSON files.
  • Hypertension Notifications (iOS 26.2 or later) join the Health Notifications group.
  • iOS asks for these only when you choose them, and they're not included in AI summaries or Ask AI.

Version 2.1

November 22, 2026

  • Health Notifications: a new group in "Pick my own" exports the notifications Apple Health recorded (high and low heart rate, irregular rhythm, cardio fitness, walking steadiness, sleep apnea, noise and headphone notifications), one record per notification with the details Apple includes. The group also has AFib History, Walking Steadiness and Breathing Disturbances. iOS asks for these only when you choose them, and they're not included in AI summaries or Ask AI.
  • "Run Task" in Shortcuts now says how many GPX files were sent, and "Export Workout Routes" appears in Siri and Spotlight suggestions.
  • Charts no longer draw a bar when the day's value shows as 0.
  • Choosing Workout Routes now asks before deselecting your other data, and vice versa.

Version 2.0

November 19, 2026

  • Workout routes: choose Raw data, then Workout Routes in the Workouts group, and get one GPX file per outdoor workout, ready to import into your fitness app. Works in manual export, in tasks and with the new "Export Workout Routes" shortcut. iOS asks for routes only when you choose them, and Wish Genie doesn't use Location Services.
  • Shared only the last 30 days with Wish Genie? AI summaries now mark the days Apple Health didn't share as "not shared" instead of treating them as missing, and don't calculate a change for those kinds. Manual export shows a note under the dates, with how to share everything.

Version 1.9

November 18, 2026

  • New step-by-step setup: tap "Give Genie a task" and Genie asks one thing at a time (what it's for, which data, where to send it, how often), then shows the finished task for you to confirm.
  • Prefer everything on one screen? Tap "Show all settings" on the first step. Editing an existing task works as before.

Version 1.8

November 17, 2026

  • Designed for iPhone Duo: open it and the home screen shows your numbers and your tasks side by side, and the task editor puts the sentence next to its choices. Columns stay clear of the fold when it's half open.
  • New large widget: your three numbers plus today's trend.
  • New AI summary tasks now start as Markdown.
  • The "Give Genie a task" button no longer covers your task list.
  • Close and Cancel buttons are now icons, like the rest of iOS.

Version 1.7

November 15, 2026

  • New data: Stand Hours, Mindful Minutes, State of Mind, Activity Rings and Cycle Tracking (menstruation, spotting, ovulation tests and more). Cycle tracking and State of Mind are asked for only when you choose them, and cycle tracking is never put in AI summaries or sent to Ask AI.
  • Also save a latest copy: tasks with hourly or daily values or an AI summary can also save the same file under a fixed name, so your own tools can always read the newest one.
  • If a file can't be saved to iCloud Drive, the task now says so instead of showing a network problem.
  • Fixed: the data list no longer jumps when you tap an item.

Version 1.6

November 14, 2026

  • New: Ask AI. Ask an AI model questions about a summary of your Health data, using your own OpenRouter account. Nothing is sent until you agree, conversations stay on your iPhone, and you can save one as a report. Included in Basic.
  • Daily and weekly tasks now send at the time you pick, and weekly tasks on the day you pick. You can change it anytime.

Version 1.5

November 13, 2026

  • Schedules keep their times: a late run no longer pushes later runs back, and missed runs are caught up once.
  • Opening Wish Genie now sends every task that's due.
  • New "Remind me if it doesn't send": a task can notify you when it's an hour late, for example because your iPhone stayed locked.
  • New "Catch up" button on the widget.
  • New "Resend recent data": send the last day, 7 days or 30 days again when the receiving end missed something.
  • "Select all" when choosing data.
  • AI summaries in Markdown start with a short note for your AI assistant: look at trends, don't diagnose, suggest a doctor if something looks concerning. You can turn it off.
  • AI summary JSON now has each day's lowest and highest value for heart rate and other averaged data.

Version 1.4

November 11, 2026

  • New: more than 60 kinds of Health data, up from 14: running, walking and cycling metrics, blood pressure, blood glucose, body measurements, nutrition, sound levels, time in daylight and more. The data list is now grouped.
  • New: workouts. Each workout is exported with its type, start and end time, plus distance, energy and heart rate when recorded. Workout routes aren't read.
  • Hourly and daily values and AI summaries now include the new data and workouts.
  • CSV files have 6 new workout columns at the end; the existing columns are unchanged.
  • After updating, the home screen offers to allow the new kinds of data. You choose which ones Genie can read.
  • A task's "Sent" line now shows what was actually in the file, even if you change the task later.

Version 1.3

November 10, 2026

  • New: choose what goes in the file. Besides every reading, you can export hourly or daily values, or an AI summary: the last 7, 30 or 90 days compared with the days before, as Markdown or JSON, easy for an AI assistant app to read.
  • Tasks can send hourly or daily values, or an AI summary every day or every week.
  • Genie doesn't connect to any AI service. You share the file yourself, or a task sends it to your own cloud or server.
  • Siri's reply to "Run Task" now says what was sent.

Version 1.2

November 9, 2026

  • New destination: iCloud Drive. Genie saves your exports to the Wish Genie folder in your own iCloud Drive, where you can open them in the Files app.
  • When a task has nothing new to send, its card still shows how many samples it sent last time, and when.
  • If a number on the home screen shows "—", a short note explains there's no data for it yet today (for example, your watch was off).
  • The widget keeps the three numbers you chose, even while it waits for fresh data.
  • Small fixes to how Genie keeps your cloud accounts signed in.

Version 1.1

November 8, 2026

  • New destinations: Dropbox, Google Drive and OneDrive (personal accounts). Sign in once and Genie saves your exports to its own folder there.
  • Choose the three numbers on the home screen and the widget.
  • A Health data card in Settings: allow Health access if you skipped it, or see where to change what Genie can read.
  • Clearer messages when an export fails, with a button that takes you to the fix.
  • Exported files are now named "WishGenie-…".

Version 1.0

November 7, 2026

First release.
