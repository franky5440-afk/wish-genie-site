Privacy Policy

Wish Genie: Health Export · Last updated: November 8, 2026

The short version

We don't collect any data. Wish Genie reads health data on your iPhone and sends it only to the destinations you set up yourself. We don't run servers, we don't use analytics or advertising, and the app contains no third-party tracking code.

Health data

  • With your permission, the app reads data from the Health app (for example heart rate, steps and sleep). You choose which types to allow in iOS Settings, and you can change that at any time.
  • The app only reads. It never writes to the Health app.
  • Health data is sent only when you give Genie a task (an export) or start a manual export, and only to the destination you picked: a Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive account you connect, a REST API endpoint you enter, or a file you share. Those destinations are run by you or by a service you chose, and their own privacy terms apply.
  • We never receive, see or store your health data, and we don't use it for advertising, marketing or data mining.

Dropbox, Google Drive and OneDrive

  • You sign in on the service's own page, shown by iOS. The app never sees your password.
  • The app asks only for access to its own folder: Dropbox › Apps › Wish Genie, a "Wish Genie" folder in Google Drive (the app can only see files it created), or OneDrive › Apps › Wish Genie. When you connect, it also reads your account's email address, only to show which account is connected.
  • Exports go straight from your iPhone to your account at that service. The service's own privacy policy applies to the files stored there.
  • "Disconnect" removes the sign-in from your iPhone; files already sent stay in your account. To revoke access completely, also remove Wish Genie from the connected apps in your account settings at that service.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Settings (your tasks, destination names and hosts, export history): stored in the app's private storage. Like other app data, they can be included in your iPhone backup.
  • Secrets (API keys, secret headers, URLs that contain a token, and the sign-in and account email for Dropbox, Google Drive and OneDrive): stored in the iPhone Keychain, available only on this device and not synced to iCloud Keychain.
  • Home Screen widget: today's summary values (for example total steps and the latest heart rate) are saved so the widget can show them. This file is excluded from backups, and the values are hidden while your iPhone is locked.

Purchases

Purchases and the free trial are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details. The app checks your purchases on the device using Apple's StoreKit.

Children

The app is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
