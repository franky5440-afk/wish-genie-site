Frequently asked questions

Some data shows "—"

iOS doesn't tell apps which Health types you turned off. Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps (under Privacy) › Wish Genie, and allow the types you want.

When do automatic exports run?

Automatic exports run in the background when iOS gives the app time, so the exact timing is decided by the system. Health data can't be read while your iPhone is locked, so a run that falls in that window waits for the next one. You can always tap "Export now" on a wish.

What does my server receive?

An HTTPS POST with a JSON or CSV body, depending on the format you picked. Each sample has a unique ID, so your server can safely ignore duplicates. Sleep is sent one row per segment with its stage (deep, REM, core, awake, in bed).

How does the free trial work?

Open the plans screen and tap "Start 7-day free trial". Everything is unlocked for 7 days. It's free and never turns into a paid subscription. After it ends, widgets and charts stay free; manual export and Shortcuts need Basic, and automatic exports need Premium.

I bought a plan but it isn't unlocked

Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I cancel Premium Yearly?

In iOS Settings, tap your name › Subscriptions › Wish Genie. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
