Frequently asked questions

Some data shows "—"

Wish Genie can only read the Health data you allow, and iOS doesn't tell apps which types you turned off. To change it, open the iPhone Settings app, tap Privacy & Security › Health › Wish Genie, and turn on the types you want. (If your iPhone was set to Chinese when you first allowed access, it's listed as 許願精靈.) Wish Genie doesn't appear under Apps in the Health app itself: that list only shows apps that write Health data, and Wish Genie only reads.

If you never allowed Health access (for example, you tapped "Not now" at the start), Wish Genie isn't in that Settings list yet. Tap "Allow Health access" on Wish Genie's home screen, or in Wish Genie's Settings › Health data, and iOS lists each type so you can pick.

Updated to 1.4 or 1.7 and new kinds of data (such as blood pressure or nutrition) show "—"? Tap "More Health data can be read" on the home screen and allow the new items. If an item still isn't turned on (some iOS versions close the list early), turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Wish Genie.

Just moved to a new iPhone or Apple Watch and the heart rate tile shows "—"? See the next question. Older days missing or marked "not shared"? See Why do I only get the last 30 days?

I moved to a new iPhone or Apple Watch and the heart rate on the home screen shows "—"

Wish Genie shows what the Health app on this iPhone has. Right after you switch iPhones or watches, your Apple Watch data may not have reached the new iPhone yet. Check these in order:

  1. Is your Apple Watch paired with the new iPhone? Open the Watch app on the new iPhone. If the watch is still paired with your old iPhone, its readings keep going there; pair it with the new one.
  2. Is "Unlock with iPhone" on? In the Watch app on your iPhone, tap Passcode and check that Unlock with iPhone is turned on. If it's off, the watch may not sync its readings to the Health app automatically, and then Wish Genie can't read them. (Your iOS version may word it slightly differently.)
  3. Is Health on in iCloud? iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud: make sure Health is turned on, so your earlier data comes over.
  4. Turn on Wi-Fi. Health data syncs more reliably over Wi-Fi; in one case the data only came in after Wi-Fi was turned on.
  5. Wait a little. Syncing can take from a few minutes to about an hour. Then open the Health app to check that today's heart rate is there, and open Wish Genie again.

Why do I only get the last 30 days, or see "not shared" in my AI summary?

Since iOS 27, after you allow Health access iOS asks "How much data do you want to share with Wish Genie?". If you chose the past 30 days and future data, Apple Health doesn't give Wish Genie anything older than 30 days before the day you allowed it. That start day is fixed; it doesn't move forward each day. Wish Genie can't tell those older days apart from days without data, so where it can, it says so: an AI summary marks them "not shared" and doesn't calculate a change for those kinds, and manual export shows a note under the dates (see the user guide).

To share everything, open the iPhone Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Wish Genie and change each item to Full Access. There's no button to change them all at once, so tap them one by one. You don't need to reinstall the app. (These are the names Wish Genie uses; your iOS version may word them slightly differently.)

Why weren't Cycle Tracking, State of Mind, Health Notifications and ECG asked for, or picked by "Select all"?

They're more sensitive, so Wish Genie asks for them only if you use them. iOS asks about them the first time you choose them in a task or an export, not when you first open the app. "Select all" skips them, so tap them yourself if you want them. If you didn't allow them when iOS asked, turn them on in the iPhone Settings app › Privacy & Security › Health › Wish Genie. In a task or export with hourly or daily values or an AI summary, the Cycle Tracking, Health Notifications and ECG groups aren't shown, because they're only in exports with every reading. (Health Notifications since 2.1, ECG since 2.2.)

What are Health Notifications, and why is there no AFib History in my export?

Since 2.1, Wish Genie can export the notifications that Apple Health recorded, such as high or low heart rate, irregular rhythm, walking steadiness, sleep apnea, noise and (since 2.2, on iOS 26.2 or later) hypertension notifications, plus AFib History, Walking Steadiness and Breathing Disturbances. They're exported as they are, one row per notification, with the details Apple includes; Wish Genie doesn't interpret them and never puts them in an AI summary or Ask AI. Choose them under "Health Notifications" with every reading (see the user guide). AFib History is recorded once a week, so an export only includes it if the dates cover the start of that week (Monday). If a kind has nothing, your Apple Watch may not have recorded it, or the feature may not be set up in the Health app.

What does an ECG export contain, and how do I get the waveform?

Since 2.2, Wish Genie can export the electrocardiograms recorded with your Apple Watch. Wish Genie only passes your own data on as it is: it doesn't interpret it and doesn't provide a medical service. The result fields are the ones Apple Health gives. Choose "ECG" in the ECG group with every reading (see the user guide).

  • What's in a row: each recording is one row with the value 1. In JSON an "ecg" object has the classification and symptomsStatus that Apple Health gives, the average heart rate (bpm), the sampling frequency (Hz) and the number of voltage points. In CSV they're the last 5 columns: ecg_classification, ecg_avg_hr, ecg_symptoms, ecg_sampling_hz and ecg_points.
  • The waveform: turn on "Include voltage waveform" (in a task or in manual export). It only appears when ECG is chosen, the contents are every reading and the format is JSON. The "ecg" object then has a "waveform" with the lead (appleWatchSimilarToLeadI), the unit (µV) and a list of voltages. CSV and Shortcuts exports never have the waveform. Each recording adds about 0.1 MB.
  • Getting times from the voltages: the list has no times. Point number i (counting from 0) is at the recording's start + i ÷ samplingFrequency seconds.
  • Privacy: ECG is only read when you choose it, goes only to the destination you set up, and is never put in an AI summary or sent with Ask AI.

What does the message on a task mean?

When an export fails, the task shows one of these messages. Codes like HTTP 404 come from your server or the cloud service.

  • Genie isn't allowed to read Health data yet. Tap "Check Health access" on the task and allow access (see above).
  • iCloud Drive isn't available. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, and make sure you're signed in and iCloud Drive is on.
  • Couldn't save to iCloud Drive. The file couldn't be written to iCloud Drive. Make sure this iPhone is signed in to iCloud, iCloud Drive is on (iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud), and there's enough iCloud storage. Before 1.7 this showed up as a network problem.
  • Can't use the folder you chose. The folder you picked under "A folder I choose" was deleted (or moved to Recently Deleted), or Genie's access to it was turned off. Open the destination, tap "Choose Folder Again" and pick it again (see below).
  • Couldn't save to the Dropbox folder. For a Dropbox destination with a folder you chose: make sure the Dropbox app is installed and signed in on this iPhone, and there's enough space.
  • Please reconnect Dropbox / Google Drive / OneDrive. The sign-in expired or was removed. Tap "Reconnect" on the task and sign in again.
  • Dropbox / Google Drive / OneDrive refused the request (HTTP 401 or 403). Make sure you can still save files in that account (for example, it isn't read-only), or reconnect it.
  • Dropbox / Google Drive / OneDrive couldn't find the folder to save to (HTTP 404). If it keeps happening, reconnect the account.
  • Your Dropbox / Google Drive / OneDrive storage is full. Free up space in that account; the next run tries again.
  • Couldn't reach the server. Check the iPhone's internet connection. For a server on your home network, make sure you're on the same Wi-Fi and Local Network access is allowed (see Can I send to a server on my home network? below).
  • The server refused the request (HTTP 401 or 403). Your server didn't accept the request: check the API key or token in the destination's headers.
  • The address wasn't found (HTTP 404). Check the destination's URL.
  • The server tried to redirect (HTTP 3xx). Wish Genie doesn't follow redirects. Use the final address instead.
  • The server said the file is too large (HTTP 413). Raise your server's upload size limit, or split the task so each one sends fewer types.
  • The server asked us to slow down (HTTP 429), or the server ran into a problem (HTTP 5xx). A problem on the server side; the next run tries again. OneDrive sometimes returns HTTP 503 on the very first upload to an account; tap "Run now" to try again right away.
  • This destination's settings need fixing. Open the destination and check its fields.
  • A password or key is missing. Open the destination and enter the password or header value again.
  • Something went wrong. Genie will try again. An unexpected error. If it keeps happening, email us and tell us when it happened.
  • The latest copy wasn't saved: … The main file was sent; only the extra copy under the fixed name failed, for the reason after the colon. See below.

Why does a task with hourly or daily values or an AI summary keep saying "Nothing new to send"?

These kinds of content only send time periods that have ended, so data you just recorded doesn't show up right away:

  • Hourly or daily values: only hours or days that have ended are sent (today isn't over, so today isn't sent), and each one is sent once, the first time the task runs after it ends. If it had no data at that point, it still counts as done, so tapping "Export now" again the same day says there's nothing new. Data you add later is sent along with the next hour or day when the task sends again (hourly values go back 24 hours, daily values go back 7 days).
  • AI summary: covers the last 7, 30 or 90 complete days, not today, and each run rebuilds the whole period. If the only data is from today, there's nothing new.

To see data you just recorded right away, use a task with raw data, or export manually.

The latest copy wasn't saved. What should I do?

With "Also save a latest copy" on, Genie sends the main file first and then saves the same file under the fixed name. If only the second step fails, the main file was still sent and the task's progress is kept; the task shows the message in red with the reason, which means the same as the messages above (for example reconnect the account, free up space, or check iCloud Drive). The next run tries again, or tap "Export now" on the task. Until then, the latest copy is still the one from the last time it worked, so a tool that reads it sees older data.

When do automatic exports run?

Automatic exports run in the background when iOS gives the app time, so the exact timing is decided by the system. Daily and weekly tasks send at the time you pick (weekly ones on the day you pick). A late run doesn't move the schedule, and missed runs are caught up once. Health data can't be read while your iPhone is locked, so a run that falls in that window waits. To catch up, open Wish Genie or tap "Catch up" on the widget; turn on "Remind me if it doesn't send" on a task to get a notification when it's an hour late. You can always tap "Export now" on a task.

Can I give my health data to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?

Yes, in two ways. In the app (since 1.6): "Ask AI" sends a summary of your data and your questions to a model you pick (models from these companies and others are available) through your own OpenRouter account; you pay OpenRouter for what you use. It needs Basic or Premium. See Ask AI. As a file you share yourself: in manual export, choose "AI summary" under "What goes in the file?", pick 7, 30 or 90 days and Markdown, then tap "Create file and share" and share the file to your AI assistant app (or save it and upload it in that app). A task can also send an AI summary to your cloud every day or every week. The summary has daily averages, the change from the previous period, the lowest and highest day, daily values, sleep stages and workouts, in English. If you share it with an AI app, that company receives this health data. A Markdown summary starts with a short note asking the AI to look at trends, not to diagnose, and to suggest seeing a doctor; you can turn it off. The summary is not a medical record, and Cycle Tracking is never included. See the user guide.

Can Genie save to a folder I already have?

Yes, since 2.3, for iCloud Drive and Dropbox. When you add or edit the destination, choose "A folder I choose" under "Save to", then "Choose Folder…" (steps: user guide). The iCloud Drive card takes folders in iCloud Drive or On My iPhone; the Dropbox card takes folders in Dropbox and needs the Dropbox app installed and signed in on this iPhone. Renaming or moving the folder is fine; if you delete it, choose it again. In the folder list, folders in other cloud services are greyed out or not accepted.

Google Drive and OneDrive can't, and they keep using Genie's own folder. Their apps don't let other apps save into them from the folder list, and Genie's sign-in to them only covers its own folder, so it can't see or change your other files. Opening that up would mean asking for access to your whole drive. If you need the files somewhere else, send them to a folder you choose in iCloud Drive, or move them with a sync tool on your computer.

Can ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini read my exports every day by themselves?

If your AI tool can read files from your cloud on a schedule, yes. Give Genie a task that sends an AI summary every day to iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive, turn on "Also save a latest copy" in the task, and set up your AI tool (for example ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, or an AI agent you run yourself) to read the fixed file name shown below the switch, a while after the task's send time. With iCloud Drive or Dropbox you can send it straight to the folder your tool already reads (see above). Whether a tool can connect to your cloud and run on a schedule depends on that tool and your plan with it. Wish Genie doesn't send the file to any AI service: your tool reads it from your cloud, and that company then receives this health data. Automatic tasks need Premium. Step by step: user guide.

Is my cycle tracking data sent to AI?

No. Cycle Tracking (menstruation, spotting, ovulation tests, cervical mucus and the cycle notifications) is never put in an AI summary or in hourly or daily values, and it's never sent with Ask AI. It's only in exports with every reading (JSON or CSV), which go only to the destination you chose. If you share such a file with an AI app yourself, that company receives it. State of Mind is in AI summaries as a daily average valence (−1 very unpleasant to +1 very pleasant) and the number of entries.

Why: first, privacy. AI summaries and hourly or daily values are often shared with AI apps or used in automations, and cycle tracking is among the most sensitive Health data, so it stays out of them. Exports with every reading are your own full copy, and you decide who sees them. Second, most cycle tracking data is a state (flow light, medium or heavy, an ovulation test negative or positive, spotting or not), not a number, so a total or an average wouldn't mean anything. State of Mind has a score from −1 to +1 that can be averaged, so it is in summaries.

That's also why the Cycle Tracking group isn't in the data list when a task or manual export is set to hourly or daily values or an AI summary. To export cycle tracking, set what goes in the file to every reading first, then choose it after Mental Wellbeing in the list.

Will the AI company use what I send to train its models?

It depends on the model. When you use Ask AI, your summary and questions go to OpenRouter and to the company behind the model you chose, and their own privacy policies apply. Some of those companies may keep what you send and use it to train their models. In your OpenRouter account, open Settings › Privacy to turn off providers that may train on your data (there are separate switches for paid and free models). Some models may then be unavailable. With "Add sources to answers" on, the search words also go to Exa, under its own privacy policy. Wish Genie itself never receives your data or your conversations.

Can Ask AI show where an answer comes from?

Yes, since 2.4. When you start a conversation, turn on "Add sources to answers" below the model. The model then searches a fixed list of medical sites (the NIH, MedlinePlus, the CDC, the WHO, Mayo Clinic, the American Heart Association, the NHS and Cleveland Clinic) and links to what it used. It costs more: each question about 3–9 times as much (about US$0.02–0.04 more), charged to your OpenRouter account. The search words go to the search service Exa, and the AI is told not to put your values or other personal details in them. Some models can't search; the switch is greyed out for them. Others, especially free models and routers, may still answer without sources; the recommended models are the most dependable. Links that didn't come from the search are shown as plain text, so a link you can tap was really found by the search. Sources are usually in English. A source doesn't make an answer medical advice: talk to a doctor about anything concerning. Details: user guide.

What do the Ask AI messages mean?

  • Your OpenRouter balance is too low. Add credits on openrouter.ai (Settings › Credits), then try again.
  • Your OpenRouter connection no longer works. The key was deleted on openrouter.ai or stopped working. Tap "Connect again".
  • Too many requests right now. Wait a moment and try again. Free models hit this often.
  • This model isn't available right now. Pick another model or try again later.
  • The request was turned down. Try a shorter question, fewer days of data, or another model.
  • The model didn't answer. Ask again or pick another model.
  • Couldn't reach OpenRouter. Check your internet connection. A very long answer that takes more than two minutes can also end this way; try fewer days of data.

How do I stop using Ask AI?

In Wish Genie, open Settings › AI assistant. "Disconnect" removes the key from this iPhone; to delete it from your OpenRouter account too, tap "Open the OpenRouter Keys page" and delete the key named "Wish Genie" (the app can't do this for you). "Withdraw agreement" stops anything from being sent until you agree again, and "Delete all conversations" removes your saved conversations from this iPhone.

Can I export my workouts?

Yes, since 1.4. Choose "Workouts" (the last group) in a task or in manual export. Each workout is one row: its length in minutes, the activity (for example running or traditionalStrengthTraining), and, when they were recorded, distance, active energy, average and maximum heart rate, and whether it was indoors. Hourly and daily values count the number of workouts and their total minutes, and an AI summary lists them per activity. Since 2.0 you can also export workout routes as GPX (see the next question). See the user guide.

How do I export my workout routes (GPX)?

Since 2.0. In manual export, choose raw data, then Workout Routes in the Workouts group (the last group). The format becomes GPX and you get one .gpx file per workout, which you can import into Strava, Garmin Connect or another fitness app that accepts GPX. For automatic exports, give Genie a task with only Workout Routes; in Shortcuts, use "Export Workout Routes".

  • Why on their own? A GPX file is a map track, not a table, so routes can't share a file with other kinds of data. Choosing Workout Routes clears the other kinds, and choosing another kind clears Workout Routes.
  • "There are no workout routes in this date range"? Only outdoor workouts recorded with GPS have a route. Indoor workouts (for example strength training or an indoor walk) don't, so they don't produce a file.
  • Location: routes contain the places you went. iOS asks for Workout Routes the first time you choose it. Wish Genie reads routes from Health only; it doesn't use Location Services. Routes go only where you export them and are never put in an AI summary or Ask AI.

Why are Activity Rings, stand hours or mindful minutes different from the Health app?

  • Move ring with a Move time goal: if your Move ring uses Move time (minutes) instead of calories as its goal, "rings closed" doesn't count the Move ring, because Genie compares only active calories with a calorie goal. The Exercise and Stand rings still count.
  • Activity Rings show up later: they're read only when a task runs or you export manually; iOS doesn't wake the app when they change.
  • Stand Hours: when more than one device (for example your iPhone and Apple Watch) recorded the same hour, totals can count that hour more than once.
  • Mindful Minutes: a session that goes past midnight counts on the day it started.

Does Wish Genie work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Since 1.8, when you unfold it the home screen shows your health tiles and your tasks side by side, and the task editor shows the sentence on the left and the choices on the right. When it's half open, the two columns stay on either side of the fold. On the outer display, Wish Genie looks the same as on other iPhones.

Where did the task sentence go when I tap "Give Genie a task"?

Since 1.9, a new task is set up step by step: what it's for, which data, where to send it and how often. The last step shows the same task sentence, and you can still tap any highlighted word there to change it. If you prefer the full editor, tap "Show all settings" on the first step. Editing an existing task opens the full editor as before.

Why does a new AI summary task start as Markdown?

Since 1.8, when you switch a task between every reading, hourly or daily values and an AI summary, the format changes to the new content's default (JSON for readings and values, Markdown for an AI summary). Switching between the same kind, for example from 7 to 30 days, keeps the format you picked. You can still choose JSON for an AI summary.

Why do hourly or daily files repeat values I already received?

Watches often sync to the Health app hours later, so a task with hourly or daily values sends the last 24 hours (hourly) or the last 7 days (daily) again on every run, with up-to-date numbers. If you store them, replace earlier values that have the same data type and start time instead of adding them again.

What does my server receive?

A POST request (HTTPS, or http on your home network) with a JSON, CSV or Markdown body, depending on what goes in the file and the format you picked. Each sample has a unique ID, so your server can safely ignore duplicates. Sleep is sent one row per segment with its stage: deep, rem, core, awake, inBed, or unspecified (asleep, but no stage was recorded). Workouts are one row per workout; in JSON the details are in a "workout" object, and a CSV file has six columns workout_activity, workout_distance_m, workout_energy_kcal, workout_avg_hr, workout_max_hr and workout_indoor (empty for other kinds of data), followed by 14 columns for the kinds added in 1.7 (category, cycle_start, mood_ and rings_ columns; see the user guide). They come after the earlier columns, which haven't changed, so a server that reads columns by name keeps working.

Can I send to a server on my home network?

Yes. Besides HTTPS, the app accepts plain http:// for addresses on your own network: a name ending in .local (for example http://my-nas.local:8080), a private IP address (10.x.x.x, 172.16–31.x.x or 192.168.x.x), or localhost. Any other address needs HTTPS. The first time, iOS asks whether Wish Genie may connect to devices on your local network; tap Allow. If that first export fails while the question is on screen, tap "Export now" again. You can change this later in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.

How does the free trial work?

Open the plans screen and tap "Start 7-day free trial". Everything is unlocked for 7 days. It's free and never turns into a paid subscription. After it ends, widgets and charts stay free; manual export and Shortcuts need Basic, and automatic exports need Premium.

I bought a plan but it isn't unlocked

Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I cancel Premium Yearly?

In iOS Settings, tap your name › Subscriptions › Wish Genie. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
