User Guide

Wish Genie: Health Export 1.4 · iPhone

Wish Genie reads data from the Health app (more than 60 kinds, plus workouts) and sends it where you choose: iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive (personal accounts), your own REST API, or a file you share yourself. It can also turn your data into hourly or daily values, or a summary that an AI assistant app can read. It has no account and no server of its own. Get it on the App Store.

1. Allow Health access

The first time you open the app, tap "Allow Health access". iOS lists each kind of Health data so you can choose what Genie reads. If you skipped this, tap "Allow Health access" on the home screen or in Settings › Health data.

iOS shows that list only once. To change it later, open the iPhone Settings app › Privacy & Security › Health › Wish Genie. Genie only reads Health data; it never writes to it.

Updated from an earlier version? Version 1.4 can read many new kinds of data, so the home screen shows "More Health data can be read". Tap the button and iOS lists only the kinds you haven't answered yet; turn on the ones you want.

2. Add a destination

A destination is a place your tasks send data to. Open "Manage destinations" on the home screen (or Settings › Destinations) and tap "Add a destination".

  • iCloud Drive: no sign-in needed. Genie saves files in the Wish Genie folder of your own iCloud Drive, so you can open them in the Files app (iCloud Drive › Wish Genie). iCloud Drive must be on: iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud.
  • Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive: tap "Connect account" and sign in. Genie saves files in its own folder: Apps › Wish Genie in Dropbox and OneDrive, and a "Wish Genie" folder in Google Drive. It can't see your other files. OneDrive works with personal Microsoft accounts only, not work or school accounts.
  • REST API: enter the URL. Add headers such as an API key under "Headers". If the URL itself contains a token, turn on "URL contains a secret" and the full URL is kept in the iPhone Keychain. Plain http:// is allowed only for addresses on your own network (see Support).

To disconnect a cloud account, open the destination and tap "Disconnect". You can also remove Wish Genie's access from the service's own account settings.

3. Give Genie a task

Tap "Give Genie a task". A task is one sentence, for example "Send every reading of Heart Rate as JSON to Home NAS every hour." Tap any highlighted word to change the data, what goes in the file (every reading, hourly or daily values, or an AI summary; see section 6), the format (JSON or CSV; Markdown or JSON for an AI summary), the destination or how often it runs (every 15 or 30 minutes, every hour, every 6 or 12 hours, every day or every week; an AI summary runs every day or every week). Then tap "Hand it to Genie".

Genie won't send anything until you give it a task. Each run sends only data that is new since the last successful run.

  • Export now: open the task and tap "Export now" to send new data right away.
  • Pause: turn the task's switch off. "Export now" still works.
  • Run now: shown on the home screen when a task failed, to try again immediately.

4. When automatic exports run

Automatic exports run in the background when iOS gives the app time, so the exact timing is decided by the system and can be later than the schedule. Health data can't be read while your iPhone is locked, so a run that falls in that window waits for the next one. If a task shows an error, see what the messages mean.

5. What gets exported

More than 60 kinds of Health data, grouped the same way as in the app:

  • Activity: Steps, Walking + Running Distance, Flights Climbed, Active Energy, Resting Energy, Exercise Minutes, Stand Minutes, Move Minutes, Physical Effort, Swimming Distance, Swimming Strokes, Wheelchair Distance, Wheelchair Pushes, Time in Daylight
  • Running: Running Speed, Running Power, Running Stride Length, Vertical Oscillation, Ground Contact Time
  • Walking: Walking Speed, Walking Step Length, Walking Asymmetry, Double Support Time, Stair Speed: Up, Stair Speed: Down, Six-Minute Walk
  • Cycling: Cycling Distance, Cycling Speed, Cycling Power, Cycling Cadence, Cycling Functional Threshold Power
  • Heart: Heart Rate, Resting Heart Rate, Heart Rate Variability, VO₂ Max, Walking Heart Rate Average, Cardio Recovery, Systolic Blood Pressure, Diastolic Blood Pressure
  • Body: Blood Oxygen, Respiratory Rate, Weight, Body Temperature, Height, Body Fat Percentage, Lean Body Mass, Body Mass Index, Waist Circumference, Blood Glucose
  • Nutrition: Dietary Energy, Protein, Carbohydrates, Total Fat, Dietary Sugar, Fiber, Sodium, Caffeine, Water
  • Hearing: Environmental Sound Levels, Headphone Audio Levels
  • Sleep and Workouts

Workout routes (GPS and location) aren't read.

  • JSON or CSV. Every sample has a unique ID, so your server or script can safely ignore duplicates.
  • Fields: each sample has type, start, end, value, unit, source and uuid. Sleep is one row per segment, with the minutes as its value and a stage (deep, REM, core, awake, in bed).
  • Workouts: one row per workout, with its length in minutes as the value. It also has the activity (for example running) and, when they were recorded, the distance in meters, active energy in kcal, average and maximum heart rate, and whether it was indoors. In JSON these are in a "workout" object; a CSV file always ends with six workout_ columns, which are empty for other kinds of data. The earlier CSV columns haven't changed.
  • Units are fixed for each kind of data, for example mmHg for blood pressure, mg/dL for blood glucose and cm for height. Percentages are from 0 to 100.
  • File names look like WishGenie-<task>-<time in UTC>.json. A REST API receives the same content as the request body.

6. Hourly and daily values, and AI summaries

Instead of every reading, a manual export or a task can put one of these in the file (choose it under "What goes in the file?"):

  • Hourly or Daily: one value per data type for each hour or each day. Data that adds up, such as steps, distances, energy, exercise minutes, time in daylight and nutrition, is a total. Workouts are the total minutes and the number of workouts (JSON also lists them per activity). Sleep is minutes per stage (asleep, deep, REM, core, awake, in bed); in daily values, a night counts on the day you woke up. Everything else has the average, lowest and highest value and how many readings there were. JSON or CSV, with no device names or sample IDs.
  • AI summary: the last 7, 30 or 90 complete days (today isn't included), compared with the same number of days before that. For each data type: the daily average, the change in %, the lowest and highest day and how many days had data, plus a table of daily values for the kinds that had data, the average sleep stages, and the number and total time of workouts per activity. Markdown (easy for AI assistant apps and people to read) or JSON. The file is written in English and is not a medical record.

Wish Genie doesn't connect to any AI service. It only makes the file, and you decide where it goes. To ask an AI assistant app about your data, share the file to that app from manual export, or upload it from the folder your task sends it to. If you share it with an AI app, that company receives this health data.

  • Tasks with hourly or daily values send an hour or a day only after it has ended. Watches often sync late, so each run also sends the last 24 hours (hourly) or the last 7 days (daily) again with up-to-date numbers. If your server or script keeps them, replace earlier values that have the same data type and start time instead of adding them again.
  • AI summary tasks run every day or every week, and every run sends a whole summary. If there's no data for the period, nothing is sent.

7. Manual export

Tap the share button on the home screen ("Export once"), choose "What goes in the file?" (raw data, hourly, daily or an AI summary), "Which data?" and "Which days?" (an AI summary uses the last 7, 30 or 90 complete days instead), pick a file format, then tap "Create file and share" to save the file to Files, AirDrop it or send it anywhere. Manual export needs Basic or Premium.

8. Shortcuts and Siri

In the Shortcuts app, Wish Genie adds two actions: "Export Health Data" (returns a JSON or CSV file of raw readings for today, yesterday, or the last 7 or 30 days, for any of the kinds of data above) and "Run Task" (sends a task's new data now). You can also ask Siri. Shortcuts need Basic or Premium.

9. Home screen and widgets

The home screen shows three tiles, one large and two small (heart rate, steps and sleep to start). To change them, open Settings › Tiles, tap a tile and pick what it shows. Blood pressure, height, nutrition and workouts can't be shown on a tile. Your Home Screen widgets show the same three. Readings you take only now and then, such as weight or VO₂ Max, show the most recent value from the last 30 days with its date. Widget values are hidden while your iPhone is locked.

10. Plans

  • Free: Home Screen widgets and charts.
  • 7-day free trial: tap "Start 7-day free trial" on the plans screen. It starts only when you tap it and never turns into a paid subscription.
  • Basic (one-time purchase): manual export, including hourly, daily and AI summary files, and Shortcuts.
  • Premium (lifetime purchase or yearly subscription): automatic exports (any file contents, including AI summaries) to iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive or your REST API, plus everything in Basic.

Bought on another device or reinstalled? Tap Settings › "Restore Purchases".

11. Privacy

We don't collect any data. Your health data goes straight from your iPhone to the destinations you set up. Cloud sign-ins, secret headers and secret URLs are stored in the iPhone Keychain on this device only. Wish Genie never sends your data to an AI service; an AI summary goes only where you share or send it. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
