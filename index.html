Your health data, sent only where you choose

Wish Genie: Health Export is an iPhone app that reads data from the Health app (more than 60 kinds, plus workouts) and sends it where you choose: iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive (personal accounts) or your own REST API endpoint, as JSON or CSV files (AI summaries as Markdown or JSON), on a schedule or whenever you like. It can also make hourly or daily values, or a summary of the last 7, 30 or 90 days that you can share with an AI assistant app, or ask an AI model about in the app with your own OpenRouter account. There is no account to create, and your data never passes through our servers. We don't run any.
