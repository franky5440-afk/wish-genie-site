Your health data, sent only where you choose

Wish Genie: Health Export is an iPhone app that reads data from the Health app and sends it where you choose. There is no account to create, and your data never passes through our servers. We don't run any.

Download on the App StoreUser guide

Give Genie a task

Send Health data (more than 80 kinds, plus workouts) as JSON or CSV to iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive or your own REST API, on a schedule or whenever you like.

OneDrive works with personal accounts. AI summaries are saved as Markdown or JSON.

Hourly and daily values, and AI summaries

A summary an AI assistant app can read

  • Hourly or daily values

    Genie can turn your data into hourly or daily values.

  • The last 7, 30 or 90 days

    A summary you can share with an AI assistant app, or ask an AI model about in the app with your own OpenRouter account.

  • A latest copy, one file name

    A task can also keep a latest copy under a fixed file name, so your own AI tools can read it on a schedule.

No account.
No servers.

  • We don't collect any data.
  • Your data never passes through our servers. We don't run any.
  • Genie only reads Health data; it never writes to it.
